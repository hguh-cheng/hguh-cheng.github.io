Schubert calculus · a worked degeneration

Two flags collide, and an intersection splits in two

A step-by-step tour of the geometric Littlewood–Richardson rule on the Grassmannian G(2,4) — the lines of projective 3-space. We start with the lines meeting two skew lines, then let one flag drift into the other. Watch the cycle stay whole, then break cleanly into two Schubert varieties.

After Ravi Vakil, “A Geometric Littlewood–Richardson Rule” — Figure 3 & Figure 2, the case n=4, k=2, α=β={2,4}.
